Version 2.1 (build 7). No sign-in, network connection, ads, or in-app purchase.

The app opens on an animated mountain Play screen. Tap Let’s play to start Classic; it is the only mode. Human player 1 starts, computer player 2 is the default. Players & board offers 16 computer levels with human portraits, names, and optional dialogue (off by default). Difficulty adjusts slightly after a completed human-versus-computer win or loss. Both-computer games wait for the in-game Play button.

Replay computer move is always present and becomes available after the first computer move; replay pauses the live game and never changes its score. Computer turns pause while the app is inactive or exit confirmation is open. Cancel resumes play. The How to play sheet includes the Privacy Policy link, which opens the browser.
