# Vision Pro adaptation

Mode: Operate. Keep Classic, local play, all sixteen human rivals, human-first defaults, adaptive skill and paused replay.

Use the established pine, cream, amber and mint alpine world in a floating visionOS window. First viewport: title and invitation on the left; named rival, portrait and prominent Play on the right. The game centers the board between row and column score panels, with turn state above and replay below. Narrow windows reflow into a scrolling vertical composition. Larger targets and native hover feedback support indirect selection. Board and scores occupy subtly separated depth planes. Moving alpine Canvas scenery remains behind the controls, pauses while covered or inactive, and freezes with Reduce Motion.

Quality bar: complete native game flow, readable contrast, no clipped actions, native sheets, labels and at least 60-point board targets with 12-point gaps. No immersive scene, hand tracking or room permissions in this windowed adaptation. Hardware gaze/pinch comfort and headset performance remain separate from simulator verification.
