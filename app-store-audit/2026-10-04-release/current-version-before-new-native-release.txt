Choose a number from the highlighted row or column. Each choice sets your rival's next line. Plan ahead and finish with the higher score. Solo and pass-and-play games work offline. Choose boards from 4 × 4 to 14 × 14 on iPhone and iPad. iMessage races use 4 × 4 boards and one move per message. The independent Apple Watch edition also uses 4 × 4 boards, with large number buttons, a thin points bar and separate pages for the board, scores, replay and rival difficulty. It runs without your phone. Local progress stays on each device. Native Mac and Apple Vision Pro editions bring Classic to a desktop window and a resizable floating alpine window. No ads or in-app purchases.
